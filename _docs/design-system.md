# Design System

## Aesthetic & Tone
TableHop embodies a **Warm Editorial Bistro** aesthetic—warm, tactile, and elevated front-of-house hospitality rather than a generic tech dashboard.

## Color Palette
- **Canvas / Background:** `#F7F5F0` (Warm linen stone)
- **Cards & Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` with `#E8E2D8` borders and soft ambient drop shadows (`shadow-[0_2px_12px_-3px_rgba(40,30,20,0.04)]`)
- **Primary Text:** `#2A241E` (Deep espresso ink)
- **Secondary / Metadata Text:** `#8C8275` (Warm sepia taupe)
- **Primary Accent / Brand:** `#B85422` (Terracotta / burnt ochre)
- **Seated / Available Status:** `#226343` text with `#EDF5F0` background and `#D0E5D8` border (Sage green)
- **Notified / SMS Status:** `#2C5282` text with `#EDF3F8` background and `#D2E0EC` border (French navy)

## Typography
- **Headings & Badges:** Serif family (`font-serif`, 'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif)
- **Body & Controls:** Clean sans-serif (`font-sans`, system-ui)
- **Numerals & Timers:** Tabular font-mono for clocks, countdowns, and phone numbers

## Layout & Components
- **Borders & Corners:** Rounded cards (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`) with warm hairline borders.
- **Buttons:** Rich terracotta with white bold text for primary actions; soft cream with subtle borders for secondary actions.
